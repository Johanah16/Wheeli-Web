Enquiry call: retaining wall

For new enquiries from the website quote form · about 10 minutes · in Lance's voice1
1
OPEN12

G'day, it's Lance from Murphy's. Thanks for getting in touch about the retaining wall. Got five minutes to run through it?

2
DISCOVER35
  • What's the wall holding back: a slope, a new pad, or an old wall that's failing?
  • Roughly how high and how long? Flat, sloped or a bit of both?
  • Can a small machine get in, or is it hand-dig access?
  • Have you got a rough budget in mind?
3
MATCH3
GOODTimber sleepers: natural look, cost-effective
BETTERConcrete sleepers: built to last, clean finish
BESTEngineered wall with drainage for steep sites
4
PUSHBACK4

That's more than I expected. → Fair enough. Tell me what matters most and I'll show you where we can trim, without cutting corners on drainage or footings.

5
NEXT STEP3

Let's get eyes on it. I can be out Tuesday 7 am or Thursday 3 pm. Which suits?

6
CLOSE LOOP26

I'll text you the time now and email what to expect before I come out.
